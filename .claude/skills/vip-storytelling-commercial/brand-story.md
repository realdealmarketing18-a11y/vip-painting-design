# Brand Story — Founder Narration (Fabian)

Fabian is the **guide**, the homeowner is the **hero**. Fabian never centers himself; he narrates the client's journey and positions VIP's Custom Color Scheme Visualization as the bridge from frustration to confidence. This is StoryBrand applied: hero has a problem → meets a guide → guide gives a plan (3 steps) → calls them to action → ends in success.

## NARRATION ARC (maps to the 8 scenes)

1. **Hook (0:00–0:30)** — Open on the result, then a curiosity question. *"How did Douglas and Sheri visualize over 20 custom color scheme variations instantly...?"* Fabian's voice is calm, assured, intriguing.
2. **Promise (0:30–1:00)** — Name the transformation and the stakes saved (time, money, stress). Tease the 3 steps + the complimentary visualization.
3. **Struggle (1:00–1:45)** — Agitate the pain: swatches, Pinterest, the $1,000 consultant dead-end. Fabian is empathetic, not gloating. "They were back to square one — overwhelmed and frustrated."
4. **Discovery (1:45–2:15)** — The pivot. "That's when they discovered VIP." Warmth enters the VO. Introduce the Custom Color Scheme Visualization as the turning point.
5. **Step 1 (2:15–2:55)** — Fabian walks through discovering their vision (the 6 SW colors). Consultative, expert.
6. **Step 2 + Upsell (2:55–3:45)** — Styles + premium upgrades (glass garage, stone veneer, lighting). Fabian elevates the dream — luxury depth, curb appeal.
7. **Reveal (3:45–4:15)** — The chosen palette. Fabian's VO slows; let the gold wipe breathe. "The confidence to move forward with a design they absolutely loved."
8. **CTA (4:15–5:00)** — Pride + invitation. "Imagine walking up to your home and feeling that same pride every day. That's what our complimentary Custom Color Scheme Visualization can do for you."

## FOUNDER VOICE RULES

- First person plural for the company ("we guided them," "we began by asking") — Fabian represents the VIP team.
- Confident, warm, premium. Never salesy or discount-driven. The audience earns $1M–$5M/yr.
- Spoken VO may use "FREE" as written in the approved script. On-screen text and captions use "complimentary."
- Never the word "AI." The process is "VIP Premier's Custom Color Scheme Visualization" — a service, described by what the homeowner experiences (instant, tailored, see-it-on-your-home), never how it's made.
- End every episode on the homeowner's pride, then the invitation. Hero lands the plane, guide hands them the keys.

## REUSE

Swap homeowner names, the pain specifics, and the chosen palette. The arc, voice, and beat timing stay fixed across every community episode.
